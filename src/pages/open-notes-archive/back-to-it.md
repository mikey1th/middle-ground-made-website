I’ve neglected myself, my relationships, and my sense of creativity for over month now and honestly, I’m over it. I’m over thinking about finishing a project I hate. I’m over spending all my free moments working to complete a building I’ve grown to despise. I’m over being this shed-obsessed anxiety-ridden version of myself. 

Unfortunately, this feeling of “doneness” says very little about what to do with the feeling of fear that has managed to linger. The fear that this shed, which I’ve sacrificed so much for, will somehow fall to pieces. The fear that I’ll never have as many ideas as I used to. The fear that I’ve jacked up my sense of self beyond all repair. The fear that the money and time I spent could have been used on something better. The fear that the things I’ve allowed to break can’t be made whole. 

Luckily, I’ve learned that fear grows when you’re still and shrinks when you move. So moving is exactly what I plan on doing—but instead of moving forward I’m moving back. Back to the things I know I love. Back to writing everyday. Back to date nights. Back to one-off design projects. Back to walking my dog as much as I can. Back to running. Back to reading in my spare moments. Back to the version of me I was proud of. Back to not giving a fuck about siding, frame joists, roof slopes, or flashing.
