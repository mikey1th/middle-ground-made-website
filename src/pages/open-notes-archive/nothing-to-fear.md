“Nothing to fear but fear itself” is a phrase I never really understood until I spent a month being afraid of a project I couldn’t abandon, and therefore, couldn’t avoid. 

In short, the phrase is communicating that the only thing we should afraid of—is being afraid. But why? Why should fear itself be the only thing we should fear?

Because when we are afraid we are at our worst. When we are afraid we operate out of a sense of scarcity. When we are afraid end up doing whatever it takes feel safe. When we are afraid we are irrational and inconsiderate.

The condition of afraid-ness is all we should fear because it is the very thing which brings our lesser fears to life. Nation states fear other nation states so they go to war. People are afraid of going without food so they buy as much as they can and leave others to starve. People become afraid of their neighbor and tell themselves false story about who they must be. In essence, people become afraid of this so they do that. 

Fear is the only thing we should fear because it distorts reality and becomes one of the chief originators of evil. If you let fear access your psyche you should be afraid of what you’ll do or who you’ll become. You should fear being afraid because it opens up a Pandora’s Box of irrational actions  and hateful thoughts. 

And while the traditional answer to fear seems to be courage, the only way to overcome this terrible condition is to be on guard, to hold fear as the sole thing we are fearful of. The only way to banish fear is to become disturbed by what you might do if fear ever takes hold of your imagination.
