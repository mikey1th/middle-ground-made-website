When we fear less, our shadowed colossi transform into the minutia of life. Loosing our job becomes a hurdle we can jump. Missing a meeting becomes a slight annoyance. A huge car repair bill stops being an entryway into a deep, dark hole.

What’s more, when fear is no longer our go-to emotional reaction nothing causes the world to grind to halt like it used to. When life itself stops intimidating us nothing is as serious as we formerly made it out to be. And finally, when we stop dragging fear into every door we enter, we realize how heavy it can be and, how much it used to weigh us down.

And it’s this sense of weightlessness that allows us to experience life for all its worth. Once we abandon the shackles of fear and start down the path to freedom we forget that we were ever caged. Once we learn to fear less we see with the utmost clarity, that is was never our lack of knowledge, resources, network, or insight holding us back, but our inability to address our fears and overcome them. 

So while challenges will certainly lie ahead, once fear is minimized or altogether abandoned, we face them as a new person. A person enlightened by a host of kind emotions, positive affirmations, and undeniable truths that seem to originate from Elsewhere itself.
