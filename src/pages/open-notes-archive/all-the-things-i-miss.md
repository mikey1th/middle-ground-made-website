I miss waking up early and writing about an idea I previously scribbled down. I miss having to stop a dozen or so times during my runs because I just couldn’t let this or that thought get away from me. I miss being a daily writer. I miss imagining the world of my novel. I miss thinking about ideas for dates with my wife. I miss dreaming about what else my career might hold. I miss feeling like I have a good hold on how I spend my time. I miss thinking about if I’ve been a good husband recently. I miss budgeting. I miss saving money. I miss watching as little digital media as possible. I miss reading as much as I can. I miss feeling like a present spouse. I feeling like a crazy artist. I miss running. I miss frisbee. I my one-off design projects. I miss thinking about fun websites to code. I miss thinking about learning how to edit videos. I miss cooking luxuriant meals. I miss all those dumb ideas I had for businesses. I miss being a good communicator. I miss honoring my relationships. I miss having so many ideas I didn’t know what to do with them. I miss the version of my imagination that wasn’t obsessed with finishing a shed I’ve grown to despise. 

I miss all these things because I allowed myself to forget how much I loved them. I miss all these things because I took them all for granted. I miss all these things because by not spending time on them I rediscovered how important they are to me. 

I miss all these things, but I won't be missing them for much longer.
