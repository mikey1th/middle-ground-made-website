When seeking change there are many levers we can pull.

We can pull the Spiritual Lever and pray that God would intercede. We can pull the Word of Mouth Lever and tell our friends, who will tell their friends, who will tell their friends. We can pull the Media Lever and make books, videos,  movies, and songs about our message. We can pull the Enterprise Lever and start some sort of organization to achieve our goals. And finally, we can pull the Money Lever, and pay people to do our bidding. 

What’s interesting is that each lever’s degree of  accessibility correlates to the degree that their outcome is guaranteed. For example, it’s easy to pray but hard to promise that God will intercede. Yet,  it’s hard to start an organization but doing so increases the odds that your goal will be realized. 

But, as with any rule there is an exception. There is a that is lever is easy to pull, yet offers a high degree of certainty. And of course it’s the Political Lever. To become convinced, simply consider that in America most people can vote and run for office. Most people can become involved in local politics. Most people can get on the phone with their senators,  mayors, school board, or whatever. Yet, most of us don’t. Most of us see this easy to pull lever that can offer results with a high degree of certainty  yet refuse to go near it—but why?

I believe we refuse to pull this lever because we’ve tricked into avoiding it. I think the powers that be, want us to avoid the mechanism they use to create change for themselves. I think the ruling class, who pull the Political Lever like there’s no fucking tomorrow, want us to avoid it because they know things can shift if we start yanking on it.

Those who landed on the right side of political change desperately want those who landed on the wrong side to feel that there is nothing they can do. Those who changed the status-quo want their would be detractors to think the political environment they created is an immutable force of fucking nature.

Thankfully, and despite those who benefit from the way things are, the Political Lever remains just as easy to pull, and just as effective. To start using the American political apparatus—one of the most effective tools for change in modern history—all we have to do is realize that our avoidance was formed by those who fear our involvement. To make the political system work for us, rather than against all we must do is refuse to believe the lie that we’ve been fed, and pull this lever just as effectively and just as often as we pull the rest of them.
