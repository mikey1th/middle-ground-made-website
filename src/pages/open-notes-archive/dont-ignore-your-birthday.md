I used to be a Birthday Ignorer, and from my experience, the argument goes something like this. 

The Birthday Ignorer considers the day they were born as happenstance, and therefore meaningless. The Birthday Ignorer in turn doesn’t expect texts from friends, phone calls from mentors, or a dinner with relatives. In fact, The Birthday Ignorer prefers the day to come and go without any acknowledgement at all. 

While this point of view is morbid it is at least capable of holding water. In some regard, we experience our birth and existence as luck of the draw. And, if cosmically speaking, nothing we do matters why waste the effort, time, and money celebrating unremarkable people and their insignificant achievements ourselves included?

There are two things I have to say about this argument.

The first is that not even the most stoic, emotionally unavailable, socially distant, Homo Economicus, homunculi really thinks they are worthless or their life to be completely meaningless. Rather, people who ignore their birthday think of themselves as unremarkable and undeserving of being paid attention to. They think the essence of their existence is extremely mid. They think of themselves as just another face in the mob of humanity and as equally unimportant as the next person. They think there is nothing that can make them worthy of the adoration of others.

The second thing I have to say is that if you have convinced yourself that you are underserving of basic existential recognition through a medium so insignificant and small as a slice of cake once a year on the day you were born I can’t imagine that you have an easy time acknowledging the personhood and innate worth of others. Sure you might fake it, but I wouldn’t be surprised, if at your core lived a cold disregard for human beings. I don’t see how you develop true compassion and care for others if you cannot agree that you yourself are just as deserving of that same degree of care, compassion, and basic acknowledgment.

So, as a former Birthday Ignorer, you should acknowledge and celebrate your birthday because there is no way positive regard for self is not somehow linked to a positive association for one’s birthday.

And of course, celebrating your birthday is just the staring place. I think seeing the day of your birth in a positive light means you are well on your way to discovering an abundant store of beautiful and amazing things about the person you were created to be.
