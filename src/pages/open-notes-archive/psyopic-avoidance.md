I keep questioning the origin of my distaste for politicians and my full-sail avoidance of the political apparatus. On one hand I get it. The most common reaction to something that smell like shit is avoidance. Embedded deep in our sense of survival is a repulsion to decaying flesh. And what else describes the political landscape better than a pile of shit ran by the living dead whose souls are nowhere to be found and whose minds and bodies are decaying before our very eyes? 

Yet, even though I agree with the sentiment, I think some of my disgust with politics was given to me. I think somewhere the thought that “all is lost” was internationally formed, rather than unintentionally found. I think Someone wants to make the American political system seem useless and broken. I think Someone wants to do whatever they can to ensure that the rest of us Americans stay as far away from the political system as possible. I think Someone wants us to think the system cannot be made to work for us rather than against us. I think Someone wants us to waste our lives trying to escape an inescapable force.

And to find the identity of Someone you have to ask who benefits from the way things are. Who benefits from the rest of us refusing to vote, or refusing to place ourselves on the ballot, or being unable to champion honest, normal, people, or believing that we could use politics to our advantage? The answer is the ruling class. The cohort of people who made their bag from the system and who want to keep it as-is so they can keep cashing checks.  

So, in essence, I think our collective avoidance to politics is a psyop created by the ruling class. I think we’ve all been tricked into believing there’s nothing we can do. I think those who benefit from the system want us to choose to distance ourselves from it.

What’s more, I think if I benefitted from the current political and legislative climate and was an indecent person I’d want you to think that the system is broken and there’s nothing that you can do about it. I’d want the very system that I use to create positive change for myself seem so repulsive and useless that the majority people refuse to go near it. Hell, I’d even encourage the masses to vote and to “get involved” so they never question my motives.

And this sinister plot hatched by those who benefit from the way things are has achieved everything it was designed to do. Most Americans don’t vote. Most Americans think politics are broken beyond repair. Most Americans get systemically shit on by the “people” who “represent” them. Most Americans are barely getting by. Most Americans don’t realize that their avoidance of the lever is exactly what keeps them pinned down. 

But seeing the strategy is only half the battle. The other half is deciding to pull the lever. The other half is deciding to wade into the shit-filled waters occupied by ghouls whose flesh is barley connected to the rest of their bodies. The other half is deciding to do something even while we recover from our psyopic avoidance.
