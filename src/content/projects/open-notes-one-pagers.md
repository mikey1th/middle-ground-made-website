Every so often I write an Open Note that I can't help but refence in IRL conversations. After enough lame attempts to get people to read my full though on this website, I began to think about better ways to share what I had written.

After taking some time to consider the best solution I landed on the ole' reliable one-pager. Now, every so often I print out an Open Note and instead of sending someone to a website I give them an heirloom. 

I'm biased towards print, but I love how these things have turned out and I love giving someone something they can hold on to.
